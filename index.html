The problem
When it is time to raise, borrow, or get advice

Your business story lives in ten places, and none of them are ready.

Financials in one folder, the team in your head, the pitch in an old deck. When an advisor or lender asks, owners scramble, then over-share to look prepared.

01
The idea

One profile.
Owner-controlled.
You choose what to share.

Business Readiness is your business avatar: one profile you build, keep current, and share on your terms with the advisors and lenders you choose.
